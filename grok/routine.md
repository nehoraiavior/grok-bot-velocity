# Grade due labels

Create a Grok Bot routine named Grade due labels. Run it once an hour in the timezone from Grok Bot settings. The routine calls the Velocity hour skill.

The routine is the clock. It is not a script. Grade due labels as part of that hour. The hour also reads the home timeline and looks up one-snapshot posts by id.

Do not post, like, follow, or reply.

A second routine may refresh the published x-algorithm repo once a week. That routine does not grade posts, and grading does not read its result.
